ANNOUNCE

Usage: announce [off | brief | all]

The game-wide news is not a channel and not a room: it is a line sent to
every player online at once, for four events -- somebody arriving for
the first time, a death trap claiming a victim, a remort, and anybody at
all rising a level.  ANNOUNCE controls how much of that you hear.

  > announce off
  Your announcements are now Off.  You will hear none of them.

  > announce brief
  Your announcements are now Brief.  You will hear about newcomers,
  death traps and remorts, but not levels.

  > announce all
  Your announcements are now All.  You will hear everything, which is
  where every new character starts.

'announce' with no argument shows your current setting instead of
changing it.

See also: COLOR, SYSLOG
